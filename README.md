# Temptasians Audition Council

Temptasians Audition Council is the shared workspace for running Temptasians auditions at Duke University.

It is designed for the audition council: roughly eight judges listening together, recording independent impressions, and discussing candidates as a group. The app keeps live note-taking quick, keeps individual opinions private during auditions, and gives the council one shared place for final deliberation.

## What it is used for

Use the app to:

- prepare an audition queue for a cycle such as Fall 2026;
- collect a candidate’s basic information;
- record each judge’s independent ratings and notes for Range, Pitch Matching, Blending, Solo, Vibe, and Overall;
- keep judges from seeing one another’s detailed reviews during live auditions;
- review completed candidates and see the council’s Overall colors;
- discuss every non-excluded candidate together;
- record the group’s shared deliberation assessment;
- place candidates in Undecided, Accepted by section, or Rejected;
- export the complete cycle to an Excel workbook.

The app is intended for a trusted internal group. Entering a name is a lightweight council identity, not a full password-based account system.

## How an audition cycle works

Lucas or Admin creates and activates a cycle, then adds candidates in their intended audition order. Only one candidate can be active in the live audition workflow at a time.

When it is time to begin, Lucas or Admin chooses a currently connected council member as the Personal Info Note Taker. That person fills in the candidate’s name, class, major, hometown, celebrity crush, and MBTI. Other judges see a waiting screen until Personal Info is complete.

After the note taker begins the Vocal Audition, every judge can open the review interface. Each judge writes their own notes and chooses a rating independently. Ratings save immediately. Notes save automatically as they are written and show whether they are Saved, Saving, or need a Retry.

Vibe stays visible in a pinned side panel while judges move through the other review sections. Grey/Unset is always allowed. Notes are optional; the factual Personal Info fields are required before the vocal audition begins.

When the candidate is finished, Lucas or Admin closes the audition. Everyone can then see each judge’s Overall color for that candidate. Detailed peer ratings and notes remain private. Judges can still return later to edit their own reviews.

## Deliberation

Deliberation is a separate panel that Lucas or Admin can activate or deactivate. Activating it does not end the live audition workflow, and it can be turned off if it was enabled accidentally.

When deliberation is active, the council can read the detailed reviews together. Every candidate who has not been excluded appears on the board, including candidates who were still Upcoming or in an earlier stage.

Each candidate belongs to exactly one board location: Undecided, Accepted / Soprano, Accepted / Alto, Accepted / Tenor, Accepted / Bass, or Rejected.

Any council member may move a candidate. Moves are saved immediately and can be reversed. The board also shows the separate shared Team Overall color. Team deliberation notes and ratings are a council assessment; they do not overwrite any judge’s individual review.

## Privacy and Access All Data

During live auditions, judges see the candidate’s shared factual information and their own detailed review. They do not receive other judges’ component ratings or notes.

After a candidate is closed, judges can see peer Overall colors only. Detailed peer reviews become available when Deliberation is active.

The Access All Data option provides temporary read access to the complete dataset, including excluded candidates. It does not grant Lucas/Admin procedures such as starting candidates, closing auditions, changing cycles, or activating deliberation.

## Ratings

- Good: strong positive opinion; very good or happy to see this candidate.
- Medium: neutral, mixed, or acceptable.
- Weak: strong negative opinion.
- Grey/Unset: no rating entered yet.

Ratings are labeled in the interface as well as shown by color. Overall colors are emphasized during deliberation so the council’s final opinions are easy to scan.

## Excluding candidates

Lucas or Admin can exclude a candidate without deleting them. Excluded candidates disappear from ordinary queues, history, and deliberation, but their profile, reviews, and deliberation records remain stored. An admin can restore an excluded candidate later.

## Exporting results

Lucas/Admin, or a user with Access All Data enabled, can choose Export XLSX. The workbook is generated from the current saved records.

The workbook contains a Summary sheet, one sheet per candidate in audition order, candidate profile information, every judge’s individual ratings and notes, the shared Team deliberation assessment, and board placement, status, and exclusion state.

Export after the audition session and again after deliberation so the council has a readable backup of the results.

## Recommended audition-day routine

1. Confirm that the correct cycle is active.
2. Have each council member enter their own name in a separate browser profile or laptop.
3. Add and check the candidate order.
4. Start each candidate and select the Personal Info Note Taker.
5. Complete Personal Info, then begin the Vocal Audition.
6. Have each judge record independent ratings and notes.
7. Close the candidate when the audition is over.
8. Repeat for the remaining candidates.
9. Export an XLSX copy when live auditions finish.
10. Activate Deliberation when the council is ready to discuss candidates.
11. Record Team deliberation ratings and notes and place candidates on the board.
12. Export the final XLSX copy.

## Important reminders

- Keep the browser open until a changed field says Saved.
- If a field says Save failed, use Retry before leaving the page.
- Use separate browser profiles when testing different judge identities.
- Do not use Access All Data during live auditions unless the council intentionally wants unrestricted read access.
- The app stores audition records in the shared database; refreshing the page does not erase saved work.
