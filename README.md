# Temptasians Audition Council

The internal workspace for running Temptasians auditions at Duke.

## Audition flow

1. **Lucas or Admin** creates the queue and starts a candidate.
2. The selected note taker completes Personal Info.
3. Every active judge records independent notes and ratings during the vocal audition.
4. Lucas or Admin closes the candidate, then activates Deliberation when the council is ready.
5. The council drags candidates into Undecided, Rejected, or an accepted vocal section.

## Privacy and roles

- Judges see their own detailed notes during auditions.
- Detailed peer notes appear only during Deliberation.
- Lucas/Admin can manage cycles, close auditions, exclude candidates or judges, and turn Deliberation on or off.
- Excluded judge accounts cannot contribute; their notes remain visible only to Lucas/Admin.

## Ratings

Use **Good**, **Medium**, **Weak**, or leave a rating unset. Add notes for Range, Pitch Matching, Blending, Solo, Vibe, and Overall.

## Exports

Lucas/Admin can download an XLSX file from the Audition Dashboard or Deliberation Board.

- **Export XLSX** includes non-excluded candidates and active judges.
- **Export All Candidates** includes all stored candidates and judges for that cycle.

Keep the page open until a field says **Saved**. Use **Retry** if a save fails.
